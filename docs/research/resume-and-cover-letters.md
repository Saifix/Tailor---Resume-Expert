# Resumes, ATS and cover letters: research notes (Oct 2026)

These notes were compiled with web research and fed into `skills/resume-tailor`, `skills/ats-keywords`, `skills/impact-bullets` and `skills/cover-letter`.

Caveat: many statistics come from resume-vendor surveys (Jobscan, Resume Genius, Resume Now), which have a commercial interest. Primary sources (Harvard, MIT, UK National Careers Service, Workday/Greenhouse/Lever docs) were preferred wherever possible.

## How screening actually works

- **Recruiters filter and search; they rarely auto-reject.** 99.7% of recruiters use ATS filters, mostly by skills (76%), education, job title and certifications. ([Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/state-of-the-job-search))
- **Greenhouse Talent Matching** highlights matched and *similar* keywords, which means semantic matching. It "does not automatically advance or reject candidates". ([Greenhouse](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/41396009937307-Talent-Matching))
- **Workday HiredScore** grades candidates A–D on how many basic and preferred qualifications they meet. Requirements need to be stated *explicitly*. ([Workday docs](https://doc.workday.com/hiredscore/en-us/workday-hiredscore/recruiter-productivity-/reference--candidate-grades.html))
- **Workday parsing:** it flattens the resume to plain text and drops header and footer content. Two-column layouts get read straight across both columns. ([source](https://www.resumeadapter.com/ats/workday/how-it-parses))
- **Lever:** if you can't highlight the text in the file, it won't parse. ([Lever](https://help.lever.co/hc/en-us/articles/20087345054749-Understanding-Resume-Parsing))
- **Hidden or white text** is shown to recruiters by the parser and reads as cheating. Greenhouse saw it in about 1% of resumes. ([Entrepreneur/NYT](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/job-seekers-try-to-pass-ai-screening-with-white-text-prompts/498109))

## Resume rules

- **Layout:** single column; standard headings; no tables, text boxes, icons or header/footer content. ([Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/ats-resume/))
- **Job title:** including the target job title correlated with 10.6x more interviews. Only use it when it's truthful. ([Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/state-of-the-job-search))
- **XYZ formula:** "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]". Give scale so numbers mean something. ([Laszlo Bock](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/20140929001534-24454816-my-personal-formula-for-a-better-resume))
- **Writing style:** action verbs, specific and fact-based, no pronouns, no "Responsible for". ([Harvard](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/))
- **The skim:** recruiters look at current title and company, then the previous role, then dates, then education. ([Ladders via HR Dive](https://www.hrdive.com/news/eye-tracking-study-shows-recruiters-look-at-resumes-for-7-seconds/541582/))
- **Skills section:** group by category, use the job description's exact terms, and give the full term plus acronym. ([source](https://hireflow.net/blog/resume-skills-section-ats-reads-correctly))
- **Length:** 1 page under ~5 years of experience, 2 pages for mid/senior (sources disagree). ([source](https://resumeoptimizerpro.com/blog/ideal-resume-length))
- **Regional norms:** UK/EU "CV" on A4 with no photo or date of birth; US resume on Letter. ([National Careers Service](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/cv-sections))
- **Formats:** career changers should use a hybrid format, never purely functional. ([Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/recruiters-functional-resume-format/))
- **Gaps:** gaps over 3 months get a plain "Career break" line. ([source](https://www.growmyresume.com/blog/resume-with-employment-gap))
- **Generic AI phrasing:** 62% of employers say generic AI resumes are more likely to be rejected. Phrases like "results-driven", "proven track record" and "spearheaded" give it away. ([Resume Now](https://www.resume-now.com/job-resources/careers/ai-applicant-report))
- **Typos:** 77% of hiring managers call them a deal-breaker. ([source](https://standout-cv.com/usa/stats-usa/resume-statistics))

## Cover letters

- **Reading habits:** 83–87% of hiring managers say they read cover letters; only 24% read every one; 36% spend under 30 seconds. ([resume.io](https://resume.io/blog/cover-letter-statistics))
- **Effect:** including one correlated with 3.4x more interviews. ([Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/state-of-the-job-search))
- **Length:** 250–400 words, 3–4 paragraphs, one page. ([Harvard 2026](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/blog/2026/08/10/how-to-write-a-cover-letter-that-actually-gets-you-hired-in-2026/))
- **Structure:** hook with a specific reason, proof stories that add to the resume, then a close. Research the company's values and initiatives. ([MIT CAPD](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/how-to-write-an-effective-cover-letter/))
- **Greeting:** use the person's name, or "Dear Hiring Manager". Never "To Whom It May Concern". ([Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/address-cover-letter-without-name))
- **Opening:** skip "I am writing to express my interest…". A referral or a result makes a strong first line. ([UMass Lowell](https://career.uml.edu/blog/2026/09/18/how-to-start-a-cover-letter-10-opening-lines-that-impress-recruiters/))
- **When they matter most:** career changes, borderline fits, and companies with 100+ employees. ([Resume Genius](https://resumegenius.com/blog/cover-letter-help/cover-letter-statistics))
